# TI-014 — Benchmark Apple Speech on the representative corpus

## Required outcome and acceptance

Run `apple-speech` through the tracked representative corpus before the provider
is included in a release baseline. Measure transcript accuracy, term retention,
native segment timing, long-form behavior, wall time, RTF, and peak process RSS.
Record that Apple Speech does not expose speaker labels or word timestamps, so
DER and word-timestamp measurements are unsupported rather than zero.
For every fixture, also record whether AVAudioFile accepted the original media
or TranscribeIt's temporary WAV fallback was required.

Acceptance requires a clean-commit benchmark record with macOS version/build,
architecture, resolved locale, Apple Intelligence availability, locale-asset
installation request state, cold/warm classification, fixture hashes, exact
commands, and sanitized transcript-free artifacts. Compare the result with the
current local whisper.cpp baseline without treating one host or one OS-managed
model revision as a universal ranking.

Apple owns and shares the locale asset. Record that lifecycle boundary and do
not delete system speech caches. Delete only evaluation-owned fixture copies,
outputs, and raw transcripts after hashes and sanitized evidence are captured;
verify those exact paths are absent and record the reclaimed bytes.

## Exploratory evidence

The 2026-08-17 [sanitized long-form comparison](../../benchmarks/results/2026-08-17-ti-014-apple-vs-large-v3-exploratory.sanitized.json)
used one private 3,234.752-second Japanese M4A and two sequential repetitions of
Apple Speech and local whisper.cpp large-v3. Apple directly accepted the M4A and
had an 18.81-second median wall time; local converted to WAV and had a
615.37-second median. Both outputs were deterministic, and normalized transcript
bigram/trigram overlap was strong, but no reviewed reference exists, so no
accuracy conclusion is supported.

This evidence is intentionally not a TI-014 resolution or release baseline: the
worktree was dirty, the fixture is outside the representative corpus, and the
Apple asset was warm. The evaluation-owned large-v3 download and raw outputs
were deleted after evidence capture, removing 3,096,993,151 logical bytes and
leaving the exact evaluation root absent.
